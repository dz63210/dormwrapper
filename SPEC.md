### Product Specs 

**User Journey** 

  * Verified email access: email plus a one-time code signs users in to a persistent account. Existing active guests can verify an email to retain their designs. See EMAIL_LOGIN_SETUP.md and SECURITY.md.
* Home Page:  
  * Click to start a new design (Primary Function)  
  * Saved designs, last session (Work in Progress)   
    * Each design is saved separately for MVP   
    * North Star: furnitures are saved in a designated room (e.g., bed room)   
  * Popular ideas (Optional)   
* New Design Page:  
  * Photo submission: upload ONE photo (at a time) of the space the user wants to decor (MVP)   
    * One photo at a time  
  * Input Interface  
    * Dimensional inputs (Optional)   
    * style preference (Required)   
    * requirements from the space(Optional)   
    * Also make the budget a range to pick from (?) that would probably simplify things. (e.g., scroll bar) (Required)   
  * Output: Populate the suggestions overlaid on the supplied photo, with a list of recommended Ikea items with links   
  * Options:  
    * Save/like the design & suggested furniture  
    * Iterate (add a warning for extra costs lol) (Button that does nothing)   
* Saved Design Page:  
  * To revisit any saved designs  
  * Photo & furnitures, with links for purchase
